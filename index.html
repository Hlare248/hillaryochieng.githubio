About Me

My path into AI and data work started somewhere unexpected: a science degree, not a computer science one. Studying industrial biotechnology at university trained me to work carefully with data, follow protocols precisely, and question a result that doesn't quite add up rather than wave it through. That habit — slow down, check it, verify it again — turned out to be exactly what AI data work rewards, and it's the mindset I've carried into every role since.

Over the past 4+ years, that's translated into a mix of hands-on AI data operations and research work. I annotate and validate data for computer vision and satellite imaging pipelines, evaluate LLM prompts and responses against strict quality rubrics, and conduct OSINT and maritime intelligence research where accuracy has real compliance consequences, not just quality-score consequences. Alongside that, I administer Airtable-based workflows to keep projects tracked and reportable, and I've grown into a Project Team Lead role — which means I don't just do the work carefully, I also train other people to do it the same way and keep a team's output consistent under client SLAs.

I bring the same care to the less technical side of the job too: clear written communication, cross-functional coordination, and CRM/customer-facing experience from earlier roles. In practice, that means I'm comfortable being handed an ambiguous brief, asking the right clarifying questions, and delivering something a client can actually rely on — not just something that technically checks a box.

My approach

I annotate the imagery for computer-vision use against strict labeling guidelines, then validate and QA that work myself before it moves downstream, rather than treating labeling and QA as separate concerns.

Annotated, quality-checked imagery data that computer-vision models can be trained on with confidence — accuracy sustained above 99% throughout.

AI / LLM Evaluation

The situation

The riskiest LLM outputs aren't the obviously wrong ones — they're the confident, well-written responses that are subtly inaccurate, off-tone, or non-compliant with a client's specific guidelines.

My approach

I evaluate prompts and responses against a structured rubric covering factual accuracy, coherence, tone, grammar and relevance, applying it consistently across volume.

The outcome

Feedback specific enough for a model or team to actually act on — not just a pass/fail score.
